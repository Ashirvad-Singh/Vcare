    Our Services

    Comprehensive diagnostic imaging and scans under one roof — accurate, fast, and reliable.

    Advance 96 Multislice CT Scan

    High-resolution 96-slice CT for brain, chest, abdomen, spine, joints, and full-body imaging with 3D reconstruction capabilities.

    Advanced Logic Totus Ultrasound

    Cutting-edge ultrasound system providing detailed fetal and cardiac imaging for prenatal diagnostics.

    Fibroscan

    Non-invasive liver scan to assess fibrosis and fat content in the liver, ideal for early detection of liver diseases.

    Fetal Echocardiography

    Specialized ultrasound for detailed assessment of fetal heart structure and function to detect congenital anomalies early.

    4D/3D Ultrasound

    Real-time imaging that provides detailed and moving visuals of the fetus, enhancing pregnancy monitoring and bonding.

    Color Doppler

    Ultrasound-based blood flow analysis in vessels and organs for accurate vascular and cardiac assessments.

    Digital X-Ray

    High-quality digital X-ray imaging for chest, bones, joints, and soft tissues with instant results and minimal radiation exposure.

    ECG & Echocardiography

    Complete heart health evaluation including electrical activity (ECG) and structural imaging (ECHO) to detect arrhythmias and heart diseases.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about our diagnostic services and patient support.

    What types of scans and tests do you offer?

    We provide 96 Multislice CT Scan, Fibroscan, Ultrasound (including 4D/3D), Fetal Echocardiography, Color Doppler, Digital X-Ray, ECG, and more — all under one roof.

    Do I need a doctor's prescription to get a scan?

    Yes, for most diagnostic tests like CT scan, ultrasound, and ECG, a valid doctor's referral or prescription is required as per medical regulations.

    How long does it take to get my test reports?

    Most reports, including ultrasound and X-rays, are available the same day. CT scan and specialized reports may take 24–48 hours, depending on the test type.

    Do you accept walk-in patients or is prior booking needed?

    We accept walk-in patients during working hours. However, we recommend booking in advance for CT scans and other time-intensive tests to avoid waiting.

    Are your machines and equipment advanced?

    Yes. We use modern diagnostic machines like the 96-slice CT scanner, high-resolution ultrasound systems, and advanced ECG/echocardiography tools for accurate results.

    Where is your diagnostic center located?

    We are located opposite Pratap Hospital, near Mini Bypass Road, Karamchari Nagar, Bareilly (U.P). You can also call us for directions or appointment assistance.
